About

Why this site exists

First Responder Law is a public information blog written for police officers, firefighters, and EMS personnel. It covers legal topics that commonly affect first responders — workers' compensation, line-of-duty disability, pension and retirement issues, wrongful death claims, and general legal news relevant to the profession.

The goal is to make this information accessible and easy to understand, without legal jargon, so first responders know what questions to ask and what their general rights and options may be.
