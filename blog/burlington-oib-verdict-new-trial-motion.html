The Jury Cleared LAPD in the Burlington Shooting. The Fight Isn't Over.

Legal News · Civil Litigation · July 29, 2025

Legal News

Estate of Orellana-Peralta v. City of Los Angeles (Los Angeles County Superior Court) is exactly that kind of case. A defense verdict in an officer-involved shooting case with a child victim and $100 million on the table is about as high-stakes as civil litigation gets. The jury delivered one anyway — and the losing side isn't done fighting.

On May 7, 2025, a Burbank Superior Court jury voted 9-3 in favor of the City of Los Angeles in the case stemming from the tragic 2021 death of 14-year-old Valentina Orellana-Peralta, killed when a round fired by an LAPD officer during a confrontation with an armed suspect ricocheted through a dressing room wall. The suspect had been assaulting shoppers with a bicycle lock while multiple 911 callers reported what they believed was an active shooter. The jury found the officer's use of force was not negligent under the totality-of-the-circumstances standard.

The plaintiffs' attorneys aren't accepting that as the final word. Their motion for a new trial argues the court wrongly excluded testimony from then-Chief Michel Moore's internal finding that no reasonably trained officer in the same position would have believed deadly force was justified — evidence they say could have changed the outcome. The City's attorneys counter that the admissibility of Moore's findings was "exhaustively discussed" before trial and that the plaintiffs had — and didn't take — multiple chances to depose him back in 2024.

A hearing on the new trial motion is calendared for August 21, 2025, and the outcome matters well beyond this one case. If the motion succeeds, it reopens questions about how far internal command findings can reach into a civil jury's deliberations on officer reasonableness — a precedent every department's litigation team will be watching closely.

For now, the defense verdict stands. Whether it stays that way is a live question with a date on the calendar.
