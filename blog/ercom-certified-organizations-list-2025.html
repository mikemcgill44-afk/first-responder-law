Which Unions Actually Represent LA County Employees? ERCOM's Certified List Answers That.

Legal News · ERCOM · November 26, 2025

Legal News

ERCOM's Certified Employee Organizations list, updated periodically and most recently reissued in late 2025, is the authoritative record of which unions hold exclusive representation rights for which Los Angeles County bargaining units. For sworn personnel, that list includes the Association for Los Angeles Deputy Sheriffs and the Professional Peace Officers Association among the certified organizations representing law enforcement classifications.

Certification isn't a formality — it's the legal foundation for everything else ERCOM does. An employee organization has to be certified as the exclusive representative for a defined bargaining unit before it can invoke the County's duty to meet and confer, file an unfair practice charge on the unit's behalf, or negotiate a binding MOU covering those employees.

The list also distinguishes certified unions from non-certified employee organizations, sometimes called affinity groups, which ERCOM tracks separately. Affinity groups can advocate for members but don't carry the same statutory bargaining rights as a certified exclusive representative under the Employee Relations Ordinance.

If you're unsure whether your classification currently has certified representation, or which union holds that certification, ERCOM's published list is the place to check before assuming your interests are being formally represented at the bargaining table.
