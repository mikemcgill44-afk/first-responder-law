SEIU Filed an Unfair Practice Charge Against the County's Chief Executive Office. Here's What ERCOM Decided.

Legal News · ERCOM · July 10, 2025

Legal News

UFC 006-25, SEIU Local 721 v. Los Angeles County Chief Executive Office (ERCOM, 2025) reflects the kind of dispute that regularly lands on the commission's docket: a certified union alleging the County's central administrative arm failed to meet its bargaining or negotiation obligations on a matter affecting represented employees.

The CEO's office functions as the County's central labor relations authority for many bargaining purposes, meaning disputes naming it directly often touch policies with reach across multiple departments rather than a single agency's internal practices — which can raise the stakes of any resulting Decision and Order beyond the immediate charging union.

ERCOM's process for these matters follows its established pattern: a charge is filed, a hearing officer develops the factual record, and the commission ultimately reviews that record to issue a binding Decision and Order resolving the dispute.

Public safety unions bargaining with the County should pay attention to CEO-level unfair practice decisions specifically, since policies originating from that office frequently ripple outward to affect Sheriff's Department and Fire Department bargaining units even when the original charge was filed by a different union entirely.
