Two Firefighters Alleged Retaliation Against Orange County Fire Authority. PERB Dismissed Both Charges.

Legal News · PERB · June 2, 2026

Legal News

Orange County Fire Authority (PERB Dec. No. 3026-M, May 29, 2026) illustrates how high the pleading bar can be at the earliest stage of a PERB case. Firefighters Juan Chumacero and John Robert Bodo each filed unfair practice charges alleging OCFA retaliated and discriminated against them and interfered with their protected rights.

PERB's Office of the General Counsel issued warning letters in both cases, explaining that the initial allegations did not state a prima facie violation. Both charging parties filed amended charges attempting to cure the deficiencies. OGC dismissed the amended charges too, again finding no prima facie case, and both firefighters appealed to the full Board.

The Board consolidated the appeals and affirmed both dismissals without leave to amend — meaning the charging parties exhausted their opportunity to fix the pleadings and the cases are over at the administrative level.

The case is a useful reminder for any firefighter considering a retaliation or interference charge: PERB requires specific, factually supported allegations connecting the adverse action to protected activity, not general assertions of unfair treatment. A charge that can't clear the prima facie bar on a second attempt doesn't get a third.
